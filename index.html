How it works

Four steps. Every day.

No motivation needed. The app makes the choice simple: work on yourself first, then your phone.

Write your problems

Type them or just say them. Sort them into sections like School, Gym or Mindset. Or tick them from 100 common ones.

17:00 it locks

Every day at 17:00 TikTok, games and the rest lock. School apps stay open.

Roll a problem

A slot-machine roll picks one, from all sections or the one you choose. No rerolls.

Show proof

Work it, add a photo or video as proof. AI checks it's real, then everything unlocks. Brick laid.

Your apps are locked

That's the screen you see after 17:00 until you've worked today's problem. With strong lock on, websites like TikTok, YouTube and Reddit are blocked too, and the app can't be deleted to escape it.

With the Windows installer

  1. Unzip and open Build Your Future Installer.
  2. Log in with your Apple ID once, inside the installer (Apple may send a code to your iPhone or Mac).
  3. Plug in your iPhone, unlock it, tap Trust, press Refresh now.
  4. First time on the iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode on (restart), then Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → your Apple ID → Trust.

After that: plug in once a week and it refreshes by itself. Your data stays.

With Sideloadly (Mac or Windows)

  1. Install Sideloadly (on Windows also iTunes + iCloud from apple.com, not the Microsoft Store).
  2. Connect your iPhone and tap Trust.
  3. Drag BuildYourFuture.ipa into Sideloadly, type your Apple ID and press Start.
  4. Developer Mode + Trust on the iPhone, like above.

Free Apple ID: works for 7 days, then press Start again. Watch the Sideloadly video

Your Apple ID is safe

We never see your Apple ID.

An iPhone only runs apps from outside the App Store when they're signed with an Apple ID. With a free Apple ID this is Apple's own "Personal Team" signing, the same thing Apple's Xcode, Sideloadly and AltStore use.

Watch: is it safe? · your Apple ID and your data, explained (with voice-over)
1

Goes straight to Apple

Your Apple ID and password go from your own PC directly to Apple's servers to sign the app. They are never sent to Build Your Future, to this website, or to anyone else.

2

Saved only on your PC

The login stays on your computer so you don't have to type it again. Remove it any time by deleting the folder %APPDATA%\PlumeImpactor.

3

Only installs this app

The installer signs and installs Build Your Future on your own iPhone, nothing else. Apple makes that free signing expire after 7 days; that's why it refreshes weekly.

4

Open source, check it

The signing engine is the open-source Impactor project (MIT), included unchanged. The installer's own source code is public too.

5

Verify the download

Compare the file with its SHA-256 checksums. The engine is byte-for-byte the official Impactor v2.7.3 release.

Apple's own explanation

Apple's developer page explains free Apple IDs: a "Personal Team" for testing on your own device, expiring after 7 days. Read it on apple.com.

The app itself can't see or access your phone, your other apps or your messages. Your problems and proofs stay on your iPhone. Want to be extra careful? It works with any Apple ID, so you can use a separate one just for installing apps. Read the full Privacy & Apple ID policy.
