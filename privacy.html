Privacy & Apple ID policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026 · applies to the Build Your Future iPhone app, the Build Your Future Installer for Windows, and buildyourfuture.fit.

In short

  • We never receive your Apple ID or password. They go from your own computer directly to Apple.
  • We can't use, see or change your Apple ID, your iCloud, your photos or anything else in your Apple account.
  • Your login is saved only on your own PC, and you can delete it at any time.
  • The installer only signs and installs Build Your Future on your own iPhone.
  • Your data in the app stays on your iPhone. No ads, no trackers, no selling of data.

Why the installer asks for your Apple ID

An iPhone only runs apps from outside the App Store when they are signed for that iPhone with an Apple ID. With a free Apple ID this is Apple's own free signing for developers (Apple calls it a Personal Team): Apple gives your Apple ID a free development certificate, registers your iPhone, and the app keeps working for 7 days. This is the same mechanism used by Apple's Xcode and by tools like Sideloadly and AltStore. Apple explains free Apple IDs and the 7-day limit on its own page: developer.apple.com/support/compare-memberships.

What happens with your Apple ID

  • You type your Apple ID and password into the installer on your own computer. The password is passed straight to the signing engine and is never shown, written to a file or logged.
  • The engine sends them only to Apple's servers (gsa.apple.com for the login and developerservices2.apple.com for the free certificate and device registration), over HTTPS.
  • To log in from a computer that isn't a Mac, Apple requires extra "device identity" data. The engine gets this from a public helper server used by open-source sideloading tools (mac-ani.jkcoxson.com). That server never receives your Apple ID or your password.
  • After logging in, the engine keeps a login token on your PC in %APPDATA%\PlumeImpactor so you don't have to log in every week. Delete that folder to remove it.
  • Nothing about your Apple ID is ever sent to Build Your Future, to buildyourfuture.fit or to any other server.

What the installer changes in your Apple account

Only what Apple's free signing needs, and nothing else: a free development certificate, your iPhone in the list of your test devices, and an app identifier for Build Your Future. Apple makes these expire automatically (after 7 days for free Apple IDs). The installer doesn't touch your iCloud, purchases, payments, photos, contacts, messages or settings, and it can't.

If you'd like to keep things completely separate, you can use a second Apple ID just for installing apps.

Open source and verifiable

  • The signing engine is plumesign from the open-source Impactor project (MIT license), included unchanged: it is the official v2.7.3 release file.
  • The installer's own source code is public: Installer.cs, window.xaml, build.ps1.
  • Check your download against the SHA-256 checksums.

The Build Your Future iPhone app

  • Your account, problems, proofs, workouts, food log and goals are stored on your iPhone (with a backup in your iPhone's Keychain so they survive a reinstall).
  • AI proof check. When you submit proof, the photos (or a few still frames from your video), the problem text and your note are sent over an encrypted connection to our own proof-check server. An AI that runs on that server itself (an open-source vision model) checks if the proof really shows the problem worked on. Nothing is sent to any other company. The images are kept only in the server's memory until the check is done, then thrown away; nothing is saved. The server keeps only a short technical log line (the verdict and how long the check took), without your IP address.
  • Forgot your password? If you ask for a reset code, your email address goes to our server only to email you a 6-digit code. The code is kept in memory for 15 minutes; the address is never logged or saved on the server. The email is sent from our address BrickByBrick@buildyourfuture.fit through the email service Brevo, which, like any email service, keeps a delivery log (with the address it was sent to) for a limited time.
  • Your saved proof photos and videos stay on your iPhone. Nobody else can see them, not even us.
  • The app can't see or access your phone, your other apps, your messages or your Apple ID. With Screen Time (paid accounts), Apple only gives the app a sealed reference to the apps you choose to lock, never their contents.
  • Optional features that use the internet: the 3D exercise view loads a 3D library from a public CDN; if you add your own Anthropic API key, food-photo estimates send that photo to Anthropic (Claude) to estimate it.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

The website

buildyourfuture.fit is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. GitHub may keep standard server logs, as with any website.

Changes

If any of this changes (for example when new online features are added), this page is updated before the change goes live.
