Changed your mind? 14 days to return

You can cancel your purchase within 14 days after you (or someone you named) received it, without giving a reason. This is your legal right of withdrawal in the EU.

  1. Tell us within those 14 days: email BuildYourFuture@exvale.club with your order number and what you're sending back. You can use the form below, but you don't have to.
  2. We email you the return address. Send the item back within 14 days after telling us.
  3. You pay the return shipping. Keep the proof of shipping.
  4. Unworn and unwashed, please. You can try it on like in a shop. If it was worn, washed or damaged, we may take the lost value off your refund.
  5. Your refund comes within 14 days after you told us, back to the payment method you used, including the standard shipping you paid. We may wait with the refund until we have the item back or you've sent us proof that you shipped it.

Want another size? Return the item and place a new order; that's the fastest way, because every piece is made to order.

Misprint, damage or wrong item

If your item arrives misprinted, damaged, faulty, or it's the wrong product or size, email us a photo within 30 days after delivery. You get a new one for free, or your money back if you prefer. You usually don't need to send anything back. After those 30 days you still have your full legal rights (the EU legal guarantee: products must be as described and last as can reasonably be expected).
